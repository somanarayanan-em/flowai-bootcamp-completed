# User Persona

## Primary persona: Employee record maintainer

Staff responsible for keeping a current list of employees and whether each person is active or inactive.

**Goals**
- Add new employees when they join or become relevant to the organization
- Correct employee names when information changes or was entered incorrectly
- Mark employees as active or inactive so day-to-day processes use the right people
- Find and update the right record quickly from a single list

**Pain points today**
- Incorrect or outdated records create confusion about who is still with the organization
- Unclear who should be included in processes and who should not
- Reliance on informal or scattered lists for basic name and status information

**Needs from this capability**
- A simple place to create, correct, activate, and deactivate employee records
- Visibility of all employees so any record can be selected and maintained
- Confidence that name and active/inactive status reflect current reality

## Secondary persona: Consumer of the active roster

Anyone whose work depends on knowing who is currently active. They benefit from accurate records maintained by the primary persona; they are not assumed to have separate tools or views in this release beyond what the shared capability provides.
