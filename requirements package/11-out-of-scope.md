# Out of Scope

The following are not part of this release.

## Employee data

- Additional attributes such as email, phone, department, manager, job title, hire date, or termination reason.
- Middle names, preferred names, or other name variations beyond a single first and last name.

## Functions

- Deleting an employee record. Deactivation is the only way to remove someone from active use.
- Bulk import or export of employees.
- Searching, sorting, filtering, or paging the employee list.
- Undo, or restoring a previous version of a record.

## Access and governance

- Roles or permission levels, such as a view-only user.
- Approval workflows before a change takes effect.
- Managing user accounts or sign-in; authorization is handled outside the tool.
- An audit trail of who changed what and when.

## Integration and reporting

- Connections to HR, payroll, directory, or other systems.
- Reports, dashboards, or analytics on the employee list.
- Notifications or alerts when a record changes.

## Presentation

- Visual design, branding, or styling beyond a plain functional interface.
- A dedicated mobile application.
- Translation into languages other than English.
