# Executive Summary

Organizations need a reliable way to keep a current list of employees and whether each person is active or inactive. Incorrect or outdated records create confusion in day-to-day work—who is still with the organization, who should be included in processes, and who should not.

This initiative provides a simple business capability for staff who maintain employee information to add new people, correct names, and mark employees as active or inactive as their status changes. All employees are visible in one place so records can be found and updated quickly.

For this release, anyone authorized to use the tool can perform all of these maintenance activities. The focus is accurate, up-to-date employee basics—not broader HR administration.
